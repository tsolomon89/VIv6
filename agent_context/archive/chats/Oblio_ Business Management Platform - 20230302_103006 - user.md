### 👤 User

Hi\! We are going to build a flutter app using a firestore database. The application is named Oblio. It blends the functionality of Customer Relationship Management (CRM), Customer Data Platforms (CDP), CMS (Content Management), Business Intelligence (BI) into one easy to use service. Oblio saves businesses months of time, technical and capital costs on the first day of use by providing a “paint-by-numbers” solution to CRM adoption, data accessibility and automations. In less than 20 minutes new users can set up an account to automatically set up or manage services like Salesforce and Adwords and many other services from one place. Oblio achieves this with a unified data framework that links the services, people, and data used in customer acquisition. To Oblio, customer acquisition is a holistic process connected by all aspects of a business. It solves the problems of customer acquisition by naming and defining all aspects of digital business management and then connecting the data across numerous SaaS and Ad Networks, for example: Salesforce, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Linkedin Ads, Hubspot, Slack, Yotpo, providing a single data view and generate automations. A clearer, easier, and more structured approach to business data management, Oblio is the solution to take full advantage of CRMs and Ad Networks for a fraction of the time and cost. Oblio’s is sold as a Software as a Service (SaaS), platform sold as annual contracts with an expected ACV of £7,800 starting.

## BUSINESS USE CASE

- Application of OBLIO’s framework to a business allows for :  
- Rapid utilization of complex processes and services as a stand-alone platform  
- Macro & Micro data management  
- Business-wide reporting  
- Easily deploy automations  
- Performance goals  
- Data driven business optimization  
- Discrete tasks generation with beginning and end

For example by integrating Oblio with Salesforce a business can clean and enrich data by automatically adding and updating fields, creating new records, data connections as well as setting automations to create and update tasks, opportunities and other Salesforce records.

When an object is a singular relation it will be called its singular name, for example “Opportunity”; when an object has many relations is called its plural name, for example “Opportunities”; both reference the same table. All objects require a unique name and a unique id; the field should be named “{object} ID” or “{object} Name”, for example : For the Products table “Product ID” and “Product Name”. Primary objects reference the object, for example : “Primary Contact” references the “Contacts” table. All Types are treated as objects
