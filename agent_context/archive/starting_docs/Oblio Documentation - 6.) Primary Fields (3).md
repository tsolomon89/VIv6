# About 

The following documentation explains the key concepts, objects, properties and processes involved in customer acquisition in how it relates to the operations of the Oblio platform.

In setting up an account with Oblio the first step is to input the products being sold. Creating products defines the properties and processes for the other objects. In understanding how Products are created and how they relate to other Objects an End User can understand the processes involved in using the platform.

# Products

## Product Summary 

Creating products defines the properties and processes for the other objects. In understanding how Products are created and how they relate to other Objects an End User can understand the processes involved in using the platform.

Products are any goods or services a company offers. This includes both monetized or non-monetized agreements. Products store default pricing, contract, and billing information. These properties set the value, duration/amount, and billing terms of a Product sale.

A Product’s Asset are Features and Solutions. A Feature names an aspect of what a Product does, a Solution describes how that Feature solves a problem. Features can have many Solutions and Solutions can have many Features. Features and Solutions are not unique to any one product. Features can include auxiliary pricing that is part of or in addition to a product’s default price. 

Products that share a Pipeline share common grouping and reporting. A Pipeline defines if a Product relates direct to Contacts or Accounts with Contacts. An unique email address identifies Contacts; an unique URL identifies Accounts.

## Product Name 

Product names have several qualities in common.

* They strategically distinguish the product from its competitors by conveying its unique positioning  
* They hold appeal for the product’s target audience  
* They imply or evoke a salient brand attribute, quality or benefit.  
* They are available for legal protection and "[trademark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trademark)".  
* They allow companies to bond with their customers to create loyalty.  
* They have a symbolic association that fortifies the image of a company or a product to the consumers.  
* They help motivate customers to buy the product.

## Pipelines

Products that share a type share common pricing, grouping and reporting.  
Products with Business to Business (B2B), Reseller, Partnership, or Investment type Opportunities require an Account; Business to Consumer (B2C) have only Contacts.

#### B2B:

* In B2B transactions, the products or services being sold are typically intended for use by another business rather than by individual consumers.  
* B2B sales often involve longer sales cycles and more complex negotiations than B2C sales.  
* B2B sales may involve ongoing relationships between the buyer and seller, as the buyer may require ongoing supplies or services from the seller.

#### B2C:

* B2C sales involve selling products or services directly to individual consumers, rather than to other businesses.  
* B2C sales often involve shorter sales cycles and simpler transactions than B2B sales.  
* While B2C sales may not require a formal "account" in the same way that B2B sales often do, B2C companies may still maintain customer databases and use other tools to track and analyze customer behavior and preferences.  
* Some B2C companies may also sell through third-party retailers or marketplaces in addition to selling directly to consumers.

# Opportunities

## Opportunity Summary 

Opportunities relate many **CONTACTS** or **ACCOUNTS** to one purchasing process.   
This process is separated into opportunity stages.  Each stage tracks **QUALIFIERS**, special **ACTIVITIES** defined when creating a **PRODUCT**. When a threshold of qualifiers is  reached, an opportunity is automatically created and or modifed.

Opportunities are the time-frame and circumstances for a Product customer acquisition. Opportunities relate Products to Contacts that meet Qualification Goals. 

Opportunity Types represent levels of qualification in the customer acquisition process. 

Opportunity Status denotes if related records meet Qualification Goals during a set duration. 'Winning' an Opportunity generates an Opportunity of the next Opportunity Type. Opportunities can have many Products, but a Primary Product defines the Qualification Goals. 

The Primary Product of an Opportunity is by default the one with the highest value. Opportunities track the total value of all included Products and Features. Opportunities allow for pricing, contract, and billing changes from Product and Feature defaults.

## Opportunity Types 

Opportunity Types represent levels of qualification in the customer acquisition process. There are four Opportunity types; Marketing Qualified Lead, Sales Qualified Lead, Purchase Qualified Lead and Resale Qualified Lead. Each Opportunity Type has Qualification Goals related to a Product. 

#### Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL)

MQL Opportunities manage Contacts and Accounts that have been identified as having a close match to a Product Persona. 

Qualification Goals for this Opportunity require a minimum persona match and should include completing website, newsletter, or demo registration, marketing opt-in, and GDPR opt-in."

#### Sales Qualified Lead (MQL)

SQL Opportunities are for contacts or accounts that have met the minimum marketing requirements. The contact has now registered for more information on your product or service or might have had engagement with specific Assets. 

The goal is now contacting via phone, email or some other medium, assigning a contact as a decision maker for the opportunity, confirming product interest and scheduling further consultation.

#### First Time Purchase(FTP)

FTPOpportunities are for contacts or accounts that have entered the sales cycle and are committed to working with you. You have already contacted, called or met them, know their needs or requirements and decision makers have been identified. 

At this stage transactions need to be completed in order to convert this opportunity to be won. Depending on your products this can be as simple as receiving payment confirmation or requiring multiple documents to be sent, approved and signed.

#### Retention Purchase(RTP)

RTP Opportunities are for customers that you require to renew a contract or make additional purchases. All purchase transactions have now been completed, but now onboarding and product experience is the goal. Customer service, support and success have activities with the primary decision makers and end-users.

 At this stage additional marketing Asset is sent to the customer. Sales tasks are assigned to manage any requirements needed to complete further transactions.

# Workflows

The purpose of a Step is to generate an Engagement Activity with a Contact. If there is no related Activity within a set duration the next Step triggers. The group of Steps to follow up non-Activity is a Sequence. Sequences can be for specific Asset Groups or for the entire Campaigns. If the Activity results in the Qualification Goals, the next Stage triggers. A Stage is a group of Sequences. The goal of a Stage is Qualification Goals related to a Campaign's Opportunity Type. Workflows can have one or many Stages. Workflows can be used in many Campaigns but Campaigns only have one Workflow. All Assets do not have Workflows but all Assets in Workflows do. 

Oblio automatically assigns Activities to Contacts and End Users. Campaign Settings, End User Settings and Workflows sets the volume of these Activities.
